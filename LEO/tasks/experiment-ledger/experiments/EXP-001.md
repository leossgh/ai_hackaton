## Why we think it happened

The scores are high across the board, which is what `her2-novel-2026-11-09` is
documented to do — it has the lowest measured interface error (6.8 Å) of the
four weights. Low min iPAE on a CDRH3 graft onto a fixed trastuzumab framework
is close to the easy case for a folding model: the framework and the target
pose are both known, so the model has little room to go wrong. High confidence
here is weak evidence of real binding.

## Why this cannot rank anything

1. **One Boltz-2 seed (42).** The gap between the two designs is 0.021 min iPAE. Nothing in this run says that is larger than seed noise.
2. **2 of 4 designs refolded** (`TOPN=2`). The other two are unscored, so "the best of 4" is unsupported.
3. **Target-aligned RMSD undefined** — the scorer reported fewer than 3 target atoms fixed in absolute coordinates. Only backbone RMSD (1.04 Å for design 2) is valid.
4. **Novelty unchecked.** `hackathon-data/` was absent, so neither CDRH3 was compared against the 525,063 known ones. Per `AGENTS.md`, novelty filtering must happen *before* ranking.
5. **`abguard` never run.** No format, length, cysteine or identity check, so neither design has a track assignment and neither is a candidate.

## What it implies for the theories

- **TH-001:** consistent with, but does not move. The designs fold; we have no affinity measurement and no novelty verdict.
- Nothing else moves. This run touched none of the other theories.

## What we would do differently

Everything the cycle in [`../PROMPT.md`](../PROMPT.md) now enforces: hypothesis
first, literature check before the GPU, novelty filter before ranking, base
validation before calling anything a candidate, and more than one seed before
believing a gap. IDEA-006 is the follow-up that fixes points 1 and 2.
