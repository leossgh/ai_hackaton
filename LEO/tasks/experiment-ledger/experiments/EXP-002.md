# EXP-002 — CDRH3 contact-free set, 1N8Z alone vs 8 structures

**Date:** 2026-11-14 · **Verdict:** `worked` · **Theories:** TH-008 (falsified), TH-001
**Literature check:** `skipped:external` — run on the sibling branch, recorded here retrospectively

## What we expected

That the trastuzumab CDRH3 positions contacting HER2 in 1N8Z would define a
contact-free set large enough to clear the `<70%` CDRH3 identity gate without
touching the epitope. If true, the hard constraint and epitope preservation
would not be in tension at all.

## What the literature said going in

TH-008 rested on PMID:12610629 (the 1N8Z structure) and PMID:31042744 (cryo-EM
ternary complex). Both give *contacts*, neither gives per-residue energetics.
That gap was recorded as the reason TH-008 sat at `low` confidence.

## What actually happened

CDRH3 is **WGGDGFYAMDY**, 11 residues, positions 99–109, identical in every
structure examined. Identity after *k* substitutions is `(11-k)/11`, so
clearing `<70%` needs **4 substitutions** (4/11 = 63.6%; 3/11 = 72.7% fails).

**From 1N8Z alone** — 4 contacting positions, 7 free:

| | positions |
|---|---|
| contacts HER2 | W99, G103, F104, Y105 |
| free | G100, G101, D102, A106, M107, D108, Y109 |

That gives the optimistic conclusion: 4 substitutions available from 7 free
positions, so the gate is reachable without touching a contact.

**Across 8 structures** (1N8Z, 22GC, 22GE, 6BGT, 6OGE, 7MN8, 8PWH, 8Q6J; 4.5 Å
heavy-atom cutoff) the free set collapses:

| pos | aa | contacts in | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| W99 | Trp | 5/8 | contacting |
| G100 | Gly | 3/8 | contacting |
| G101 | Gly | 2/8 | contacting |
| D102 | Asp | 6/8 | contacting |
| G103 | Gly | 7/8 | contacting |
| F104 | Phe | 4/8 | contacting |
| Y105 | Tyr | 7/8 | contacting |
| **A106** | Ala | **0/8** | **never contacts** |
| **M107** | Met | **0/8** | **never contacts** |
| **D108** | Asp | **0/8** | **never contacts** |
| Y109 | Tyr | 1/8 | contacting (weakest) |

**Only 3 positions never contact HER2 in any structure.** Four are needed.

## Why we think it happened

1N8Z has gaps (HER2 residues 102–110, 303–305, 361–364, 581–590 unmodelled). A
position looks "free" when its partner simply is not in the model. Several
CDRH3 positions contact HER2 residues in the 579–593 region, which 1N8Z does
not model — so a single-structure analysis systematically *under*-counts
contacts, and does so in the direction that flatters the design.

## What it implies for the theories

- **TH-008 retired.** "The 1N8Z contact residues define which positions are
  load-bearing" is false as stated: 1N8Z alone mislabels 4 contacting positions
  as free. Superseded by TH-009.
- **TH-009 (new), confidence `high`.** The `<70%` CDRH3 identity gate cannot be
  met without mutating at least one HER2-contacting position. Minimum-risk
  recipe: **A106, M107, D108 + Y109**, since Y109 contacts in only 1 of 8
  structures. 4 substitutions, 63.6% identity, one weak contact sacrificed.
- **TH-010 (new), confidence `high`.** CDRH3 carries only **20–34%** of the
  interface across structures (26% in 1N8Z). H2 contributes 14–47%, L3 11–29%,
  L1 5–17%, and framework up to 23% in 6BGT. All four HER2 RFD4 weights
  redesign CDRH3 only, so they address at most a third of the interface.
- **TH-001 unchanged but reframed.** Wholesale CDRH3 diversification is still
  supported as an affinity route (PMID:8918938), but it is no longer free of
  epitope risk.

## What we would do differently

Never conclude from one crystal structure where a cross-check is cheap. The
correction cost one script and reversed the answer. Unmodelled residues are not
absent residues — the gap list belongs in the analysis, not the footnotes.
