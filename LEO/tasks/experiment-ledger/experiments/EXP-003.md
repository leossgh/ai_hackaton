# EXP-003 — structure scores vs a sequence model for ranking CDRH3 variants

**Date:** 2026-11-14 · **Verdict:** `worked` · **Theories:** TH-011, TH-001
**Literature check:** `skipped:external` — teammate's run, recorded retrospectively

> **Provenance.** Not our run. Branch `origin/nick`, commit `aec87f4`, folder
> `NICCOLO/eda/`. Recorded here because it settles TH-011 by direct measurement
> and changes how the whole pipeline should rank.

## What we expected

That the interface scores we rank on — ipTM, min_ipae, topmodel — carry enough
signal to order HER2 CDRH3 designs by binding. EXP-001 ranked two designs on a
0.021 min_ipae gap on exactly that assumption.

## What actually happened

### Part 1 — can structure scores tell a real complex from a shuffled one?

Real pairs (`i==j`) against shuffled pairs (`i!=j`), roughly 8,000–15,000
shuffled per model.

| Model | ipTM AUROC | topmodel AUROC | real median ipTM | shuffled median ipTM |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AF3 | **0.754** | 0.397 | 0.50 | 0.18 |
| **Boltz-2** | **0.645** | 0.443 | **0.911** | **0.884** |
| Chai-1 | 0.568 | 0.492 | 0.208 | 0.191 |

Two things stand out.

**The topmodel score is worse than random, and inverted** — shuffled medians are
*higher* than real ones (AF3 25.84 vs 23.70; Boltz-2 31.04 vs 29.96). It is
anti-informative. Spearman between topmodel and ipTM is −0.182 (AF3) and −0.658
(Chai-1), so the two scores actively disagree.

**Boltz-2 ipTM barely separates anything.** Real median 0.911 against shuffled
0.884, and **7,723 of 8,190 shuffled pairs (94.3%) score ≥0.7**, against 98.9%
of real ones. A high Boltz-2 ipTM is what a wrong pair looks like too.

### Part 2 — a sequence model on the labelled data

SPR-768 (620 outcomes, 179 with quantified KD) and FACS 524k, with three
leakage guards: single-linkage Hamming ≤2 clusters held out together, every
result also reported on variants **≥3 mutations** from trastuzumab (the band
`abguard` forces us into), and the 31 SPR sequences present in FACS removed
before fitting the FACS model.

| Stage | Model | Metric | All | ≥3 mut |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Binder or not | one-hot logistic | AUROC | **0.927** | **0.915** |
| | mutation count alone | AUROC | 0.800 | 0.763 |
| | FACS score alone | AUROC | 0.696 | 0.678 |
| log10 KD | one-hot + mut count + FACS ridge | Spearman | **+0.687** | **+0.622** |
| | one-hot ridge | Spearman | +0.667 | +0.610 |
| | mutation count alone | Spearman | +0.387 | +0.242 |

The ranker's top 20 has a median true KD of **1.42 nM** against **86 nM** across
the set — a ~60× improvement — and it recovers the best binder in the set at
0.023 nM.

## Why we think it happened

Our designs graft a new CDRH3 onto a fixed trastuzumab framework with a known
target pose. That is close to the easy case for a co-folding model: the
framework and the docking geometry are given, so the model reports high
confidence almost regardless of whether the loop it built actually binds. The
score is measuring "is this a plausible antibody–antigen complex", which every
shuffled pair also satisfies, not "does this sequence bind HER2".

## What it implies for the theories

- **TH-011 confirmed by measurement, and its open caveat closed.** TH-011 rested
  on loop-RMSD benchmarks with the inference from loop accuracy to score noise
  flagged as ours, not the literature's. EXP-003 measures the thing directly, on
  our target and our models. Stays `high`, now on primary evidence.
- **EXP-001 is retroactively explained.** Its Boltz-2 ipTM of 0.946 and 0.965
  sit inside the distribution 94.3% of shuffled decoys occupy. Those numbers
  were never evidence of binding, which is why its verdict stays `inconclusive`.
- **TH-013 (new, `high`).** Structure-based interface scores cannot rank HER2
  CDRH3 designs; topmodel is anti-informative; AF3 ipTM is the least bad.
- **TH-014 (new, `high`).** A one-hot sequence model does rank them, at Spearman
  +0.622 inside the ≥3-mutation band, so ranking should move to it.
- **TH-015 (new, `medium`).** Per-position measured SPR preferences give a
  data-driven substitution recipe, but the best residues rest on n=4–5.

## What we would do differently

EXP-001 should never have reported a ranking from a metric whose discrimination
had not been established. The decoy control here costs nothing — shuffle the
pairs and look — and it is now the thing to run before trusting any new score.

**Scope limit to respect:** labels exist only for 10-residue CDRH3 variants on
the trastuzumab framework, i.e. the **optimisation track**. None of Part 2
transfers to de novo designs, where we have no labels at all.
