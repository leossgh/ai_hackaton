# EXP-008 — what beats trastuzumab inside the legal band, and where

**Date:** 2026-11-14 · **Verdict:** `worked` · **Theories:** TH-018, TH-009, TH-015 · **Idea:** IDEA-008
**Literature check:** `skipped:budget` — AMASS credits exhausted

> **Ours.** Hypothesis written before the analysis. Follows directly from
> EXP-007, which showed the legal band costs ~4× in affinity but left the
> question that actually matters unanswered.

## Hypothesis, stated before the run

Among CDRH3 variants `abguard` would permit (≥4 substitutions), at least one
beats **trastuzumab's own measured KD** — the number we are scored against, as
opposed to the dataset's best variant — and the successful ones share a
substitution pattern we can design toward.

## What we did

No new runs. Grouped the 30 quantified binders in the committed calibration
table by Hamming distance to trastuzumab's CDRH3, pulled trastuzumab's own
measured KD out as the baseline, and decomposed each legal variant into
per-position substitutions against `WGGDGFYAMD` (positions 99–108).
[`../analysis/legal_band_affinity.py`](../analysis/legal_band_affinity.py).

## What actually happened

**Trastuzumab CDRH3 `WGGDGFYAMD`: measured KD 0.152 nM.** That is the bar.

| nmut | n | best KD | median KD |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 4 | 13.000 nM | 35.5 nM |
| 3 | 4 | **0.023 nM** | 3.7 nM |
| **≥4 (legal)** | **21** | **0.095 nM** | **127.5 nM** |

**Exactly 1 of 21 legal variants beats trastuzumab**, and only by 1.6×:

| Variant | nmut | KD | substitutions |
|---|---|---|---|
| **WGGSGPYLFD** | 4 | **0.095 nM** | D102S F104P **A106L** M107F |
| WGAEGFYYWD | 4 | 0.368 nM | G101A D102E **A106Y** M107W |
| YLGSSFYVFG | 7 | 2.900 nM | W99Y G100L D102S G103S **A106V** M107F D108G |
| WGECDFYVHD | 5 | 4.600 nM | G101E D102C G103D **A106V** M107H |
| WASCSFYSFD | 6 | 9.242 nM | G100A G101S D102C G103S **A106S** M107F |
| WDAGGFYAKV | 5 | 15.366 nM | G100D G101A D102G M107K D108V |
| WTRPGLYVFA | 7 | 21.000 nM | G100T G101R D102P F104L **A106V** M107F D108A |
| WGGTGDYRYD | 4 | 48.412 nM | D102T F104D **A106R** M107Y |

The set's best binder overall — `WGSQGFYAYD`, 3 substitutions, **0.023 nM**,
6.5× better than trastuzumab — is **forbidden** by the identity rule.

### The convergence worth acting on

Across those 8 tightest legal variants:

| position | mutated in | contacts HER2 (EXP-002) |
|---|---|---|
| **M107** | **8 of 8** | **0 of 8 structures** |
| **D102** | **8 of 8** | 6 of 8 |
| **A106** | **7 of 8** | **0 of 8 structures** |
| F104 | 3 of 8 | 4 of 8 |
| D108 | 3 of 8 | **0 of 8 structures** |

**A106 and M107 are exactly the two positions EXP-002 found never contact HER2
in any of 8 crystal structures.** The measured affinity data and the structural
contact analysis arrive at the same positions from completely independent
directions. And the single legal winner's `A106L` is the best residue at that
position in TH-015's measured per-position table (log10 KD 0.64).

## Why we think it happened

A106 and M107 sit outside the contact interface, so substitutions there can
change loop conformation, packing or entropy without destroying a contact. They
are the cheapest places to spend the substitutions the identity rule demands.
D102 being mutated in 8 of 8 is the interesting exception — it *does* contact
HER2 in 6 of 8 structures, yet changing it is apparently tolerated or helpful,
which softens TH-009's framing: touching a contact is a risk, not a veto.

## What it implies

- **TH-018 gets its baseline.** We are scored against 0.152 nM, not 0.023 nM.
- **TH-009 strengthened and softened.** Strengthened: its "safe" positions are
  the ones winners actually use. Softened: the best legal variant also changes
  two *contacting* positions (D102, F104) and still beats trastuzumab.
- **TH-019 (new, `high`).** Beating trastuzumab in the legal band is
  demonstrated but rare and narrow — 1 of 21, 1.6×.
- **IDEA-008 revised.** Our recipe was A106/M107/D108/Y109. The measured
  evidence says **D102 + A106 + M107 + one of F104/D108** is the pattern that
  works, and that Y109 — which the SPR table cannot even see — is a worse bet
  than D102, which is mutated in every tight legal variant.

## Honest limits

- **The sample is not random.** These 60 variants were chosen for a fold
  calibration set (30 binders spanning the KD range, 30 non-binders), so "1 of
  21" is an existence proof, not a base rate. The full SPR-768 has 179
  quantified variants and would give a real hit rate — IDEA-017.
- n=21 legal binders, and the position counts come from the 8 tightest. This is
  a pattern worth designing toward, not a validated model.
- **`WGGSGPYLFD` itself is unusable.** It is in the HER2 dataset, so it fails
  the novelty filter outright. It is a template to learn from, never to submit.
- Binding only. Nothing here speaks to epitope preservation, which is scored by
  competition against trastuzumab, or to thermostability.
