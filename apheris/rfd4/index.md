# Design with RFD4-Proteina

> **On the hackathon VM?** There is no Foundry Hub there. Use [Run on your hackathon VM](18-vm.md) instead of the `apheris-foundry` commands on this page.


Open [the offline reader](Tutorial.html), or read [the shared setup](00-start.md)
and choose a task:

1. [Create a protein](01-from-scratch.md): generate a new sequence and fold.
2. [Design a binder](02-miniprotein.md): miniprotein, VHH or paired VH/VL.
3. [Refine a binder](04-refine.md): explore variants of a generated parent.
4. [Redesign trastuzumab for HER2](12-her2.md): fine-tuned HER2 models and how they compare.

Then [evaluate candidates](06-evaluate.md).

On the hackathon VM, start with [Run on your hackathon VM](18-vm.md).
